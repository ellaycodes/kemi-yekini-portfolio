I · AboutProfile

A practitioner turned professor

From KPMG and Deloitte to leading research centres in the UK.

Kemi is a Chartered Accountant who began her career at KPMG and Deloitte in Nigeria and held senior management roles in industry before moving into academia in 2007.

Her research examines how transparent and readable corporate reporting is, from sustainability and CSR disclosures to financial narratives, governance and fraud.

She introduced a semiotics-based method for studying CSR communication, and has worked with sustainability consultancy Radley Yeldar assessing the language of client reports.
